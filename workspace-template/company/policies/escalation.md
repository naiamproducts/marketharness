# Escalation policy — {{company}}

Starter rules. `community-triage` routes ESCALATE items here; the escalation
path ends with the founder.

## Involve the founder when

- Pricing disputes, refunds, or contractual questions.
- Legal threats, regulatory claims, or anything mentioning lawyers.
- Press, partnerships, investor interest, or interview requests.
- Any request to promise a feature, date, or capability.
- A thread is going viral negatively and tone needs a human decision.
- The agent is asked to act outside the marketing scope
  (product direction, engineering, finance, legal) — one-line redirect to
  marketing-lead, then escalate if the user insists.

## Escalation path

1. `community-triage` marks the item ESCALATE with reason + SLA urgency.
2. The item appears in the chat summary and in `community/triage-<date>.md`.
3. The founder decides: reply (goes back through response-drafting → publish
   confirm flow) or stay silent.
4. Never draft a public reply to an ESCALATE item before the founder decides.
